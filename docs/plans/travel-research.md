# Travel research (2026-10-10)

Wiki research for every way to reach every farm patch: where it lands, the walk, and every extra step.
Coordinates marked ~ are estimates to check in game; others were read from wiki map templates. Times assume
a 3-4.5 s teleport and running 2 tiles per 0.6 s tick. Source pages: oldschool.runescape.wiki (Fruit tree
patch, Herb patch, Captain Barnaby, Brimhaven, Spirit tree, Elkoy, Gnome maze, Lletya, Teleport crystal,
Ardougne cloak, Quetzal Transport System, Farming Guild, Xeric's talisman, Kharedst's memoirs, Nemus Retreat,
Pendant of Ates, Kastori, Achilka, Hunter Guild, Shortcut, ...).

## Kourend and Varlamore

### Varlamore quetzals
Built by default: Aldarin (1389,2899), Auburnvale (1410,3363), Civitas illa Fortis (1696,3141), Hunter Guild
(1584,3055), Quetzacalli Gorge (1512,3222), Sunset Coast (1548,2997), Tal Teklan (1225,3089), The Teomat
(1437,3169). Unbuilt: Cam Torum entrance, Colossal Wyrm Remains, Fortis Colosseum, Kastori (1343,3020), Outer
Fortis, Salvager Overlook. Renu (Travel) flies between built sites, free (medium confidence); the whistle
needs no Renu. Chain: Hunter Guild (Ortus herb), walk back 39, Renu to Kastori (fruit), walk back 36, Renu to
Auburnvale (Nemus tree).
