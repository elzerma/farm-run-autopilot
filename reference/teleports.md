# OSRS Farming Run Travel Reference

Source: Old School RuneScape Wiki (oldschool.runescape.wiki), fetched 2026-10-06. Main pages used: *Tree patch*, *Fruit tree patch*, *Herb patch*, *Farming runs* (the wiki's own run guide; `Tree run`, `Herb run`, `Fruit tree run` all redirect there), spellbook pages, and individual item/location pages.

How to read this:
- **Wiki primary** = the option the wiki's *Farming runs* guide lists first for that stop. This is the best available stand-in for "common community choice".
- **Walk** = rough distance from arrival to patch: **short** (about 0-15 tiles, patch on screen), **medium** (about 15-40 tiles), **long** (over 40 tiles, or needs obstacles/NPC transport). These are estimates based on the wiki's directions. Exact tile counts were **not** given on the wiki unless stated, so treat all tile counts as **UNVERIFIED** unless marked otherwise.
- **UNVERIFIED** = could not be confirmed on the wiki during this research.

### Spirit trees
- Fixed trees (need Tree Gnome Village quest): Tree Gnome Village, Battlefield of Khazard, Grand Exchange, Feldip Hills. Gnome Stronghold tree needs The Grand Tree to depart. Prifddinas needs Song of the Elves. Others exist (Incomitatus, Laguna Aurorae).
- Player-planted trees (83 Farming): Port Sarim, Etceteria, Brimhaven, Hosidius, Farming Guild (Farming Guild patch needs 85 Farming to reach the advanced tier). Number of trees you can grow at once: 1 at 83, 2 at 88, 3 at 93, 4 at 96, all at 99.
- POH spirit tree: 75 Construction + 83 Farming. Spirit tree and fairy ring combined: 95 Construction. Does not count toward the planting limit.

### Fairy rings
- Unlocked after starting Fairytale II (Fairytale I done). Needs dramen or lunar staff unless Elite Lumbridge & Draynor Diary is done.
- POH fairy ring: 85 Construction.
- Codes used below are taken from each patch's wiki page. A wiki summary of the main Fairy ring page gave some contradictory placements (e.g. CIQ described as Gnome Stronghold); the patch pages were used instead.
- Getting to a ring (`FairyRingAccess`), from the wiki Fairy ring page's quick-access table (tiles from arrival to the ring): Ardougne cloak, any tier, unlimited Kandarin Monastery teleport -> DJP, 51 tiles east past the Tower of Life; Slayer ring (Fremennik Slayer Dungeon) -> AJR, 16 tiles; Quest point cape -> BLR, 12 tiles east of the Legends' Guild. Also on that table but not used yet: Achievement diary cape (Le-sabrè) -> CKS 20, Arceuus Library -> CIS 34, Necklace of passage (Wizards' Tower) -> DIS 39, Battlefront Teleport -> CIR 42, Ring of wealth (Miscellania) -> CIP 48.
- The ring by a stop is taken to be as far from the patch as the walk when arriving there by that ring.

### Gnome gliders (need The Grand Tree)
Ta Quir Priw (Grand Tree), Gandius (Karamja shipyard), Kar-Hewo (Al Kharid), Sindarpos (White Wolf Mountain, a route to Catherby), Lemanto Andra (Digsite, needs 153 museum kudos, one-way), Lemantolly Undri (Feldip, needs partial One Small Favour), Ookookolly Undri (Ape Atoll, Monkey Madness II).

### POH (player-owned house)
- House portal locations: Rimmington (1 Con), **Taverley (10)**, Pollnivneach (20), **Hosidius (25**, must have visited Kourend), Rellekka (30), Aldarin (35, must have visited Varlamore), **Brimhaven (40)**, Yanille (50), Prifddinas (70, Song of the Elves). Boosts not allowed.
- Portal nexus: marble 72 Con (4 destinations), gilded 82 (8), crystalline 92 (41). Adding a teleport costs 1,000x the spell's rune cost. Farming-relevant: Catherby, Kharyrll, Harmony Island, Fishing Guild, Trollheim/Troll Stronghold, Weiss (via salts), Fenkenstrain's, Kourend, Civitas illa Fortis, Ardougne.
- Jewellery box: basic 81 Con (ring of dueling, games necklace), fancy 86 (+ combat bracelet, skills necklace), ornate 91 (+ amulet of glory, ring of wealth). Unlimited use. Covers Farming Guild (skills necklace), Ranging Guild (combat bracelet), Falador Park (ring of wealth), Castle Wars / Ferox Enclave (dueling).
- Mounted glory: in Achievement Gallery (UNVERIFIED level this session; commonly 47 Con).
